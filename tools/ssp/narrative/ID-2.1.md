Every person will authenticate with a certificate on a smart card. Cloud sign-in runs through Entra certificate-based authentication, and on-premises logon through Active Directory smart cards. Passwords survive in only two places. Built-in accounts that CyberArk vaults and injects are one, and the break-glass accounts under Emergency Access are the other.

The ground control stations need two adjustments. First, each station checks the CAC locally against a cached revocation list, so a station keeps working when its conduit is down. Second, a station whose aircraft is airborne will never lock an account after failed logons. It delays the next prompt and raises an alert instead, because an operator locked out mid-flight is a far worse outcome than a guessed PIN. The AMP records that setting.
