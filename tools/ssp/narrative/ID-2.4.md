A new identity will start with no access at all, and roles are what grant it. Non-privileged roles are Active Directory groups, synchronized to Entra ID, and every role includes the holder's team. That one detail keeps the payload vendor split intact. An analyst on one mission team has no role that reaches the other team's vendor data.

Partners get the narrowest view. Their release-by-nation groups reach only the partner API, so each partner sees only what has been released to its own nation. Anyone who has not signed in sees nothing but the banner and the sign-in pages.
