KESTREL checks the credentials people carry, but it does not issue them. DoD issues the CACs, and the program office sponsors performers' cards through DoD's contractor sponsorship system, asking for revocation the day someone leaves. IT Operations will reset CAC PINs at dedicated workstations in the data center and at each operating site. A lost card gets no stand-in credential, and its holder waits for a replacement.

Devices and workloads are another matter. Their certificates come from the program PKI, whose keys sit in the nShield HSMs, and Keyfactor Command manages them in every location. Revocation checks have to tolerate an outage, so services accept a cached response for up to 24 hours. The stations go further and keep a local list, refreshed daily and before each flight. Any outage longer than the AMP's grace period falls to break-glass.
