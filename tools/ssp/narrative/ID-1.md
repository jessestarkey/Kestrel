KESTREL's identity design rests on Active Directory in the mission data center. It is the source of truth for who people are, and Microsoft Entra ID carries a copy of it into the cloud. Because the directory controls access to every boundary, KESTREL protects it as its most valuable asset. The outcomes below follow a person's identity through its life. It starts on the day they are proofed and enrolled, runs through the access they are granted and reviewed for, and ends when their account is retired.
