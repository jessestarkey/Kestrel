KESTREL will proof every person before issuing an account, at the assurance level the AMP sets for their population. For DoD users and performers, most of that work is already done. DoD proofed them to issue their CAC, which also confirmed their address. KESTREL will bind each account to that CAC record instead of proofing the person a second time. The program office's sponsorship of each performer's CAC doubles as the nomination KESTREL requires.

Partners and vendors are different, since they hold no CAC. A KESTREL sponsor will attest to each one instead. Either way, IT Operations records the enrollment in Jira Service Management, and Entra ID Governance creates the baseline account once enrollment is complete. Two attributes keep these populations distinguishable later on. CAC affiliation marks contractors, while a nationality attribute marks partners.
