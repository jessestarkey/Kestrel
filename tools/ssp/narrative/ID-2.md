This capability covers how people prove their identity and what that identity lets them do. In nearly every case, KESTREL users will authenticate with a smart card, whether a CAC, an ECA credential, or a partner nation's PKI card. Access then depends on role and team, and it keeps being checked against device posture and risk for as long as a session lasts. The ground control stations follow the same rules, with a few deliberate exceptions where a locked console could put an aircraft at risk.
