The identity provider controls access to every boundary in KESTREL, so it will be monitored more closely than any other system. Defender for Identity covers the domain controllers in the data center and at the alternate site. In the cloud, Entra ID Protection does the same job for sign-ins. Both send their alerts to Splunk Enterprise Security, where the Security Team and the CSSP see them alongside every other source.

Alongside that monitoring, the directory will be hardened to its DISA STIG, and Entra ID to the cloud provider's IL5 baseline for the identity service.
