KESTREL will scan for exposed secrets in every place they could leak. From Phase 2, GitLab Secret Detection scans the repositories, pipeline logs, and container images on every build and on a schedule. Defender for Cloud covers cloud storage, and the service desk tickets are scanned too, since users sometimes paste credentials into them. Beyond the boundary, GitGuardian watches public sources for any KESTREL credential. A secret found in a release candidate fails Certification to Field outright.
