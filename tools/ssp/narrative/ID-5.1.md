All of the identity decisions above come together in one Account Management Plan. It covers every identity population KESTREL has, from people and partners to AI agents and OT devices, across the cloud, the data center, the alternate site, and the fleet. In it are the assurance levels, the role bundles, the maintenance-window rules, and the break-glass procedures.

The plan also explains the station exceptions in one place, which matters because they are easy to misread out of context. Those are the lock exception under CCSD tailoring action 2, no lockout or idle logout while an aircraft is airborne, and the revocation grace period. The Security Team's ISSM owns the plan and the Configuration Control Board approves it. Because it names the products the Security Implementation Plan selects, its procedures can be followed as written.
