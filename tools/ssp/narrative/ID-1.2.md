Two kinds of outsiders will sign in with certificates KESTREL does not issue. Vendor support engineers come first. They will connect from the first build, on External Certificate Authority credentials at the Medium Hardware level, and each vendor identity will reach only its own support cases.

Coalition partners follow in Phase 3, once integration testing under the interim authorization to test is finished. Their systems will present certificates from their own nation's PKI or an ECA, and KESTREL will trust them only for the partner API that Mission Team 2 runs. Every partner has a named KESTREL sponsor, and every external credential is checked for revocation at each session. Because the trust list names each partner nation's issuing authorities, adding a new partner nation is significant enough to trigger a CCSD revision.
