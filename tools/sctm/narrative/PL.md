# PL: Planning

## PL-1

The program manager will issue the planning policy as part of KESTREL's system security policy. The SSP and the governing plans hold the procedures. The Configuration Control Board will approve the policy and plans, and each will be reviewed at least once a year.

## PL-2

The ISSM will keep KESTREL's System Security Plan in OSCAL from its first version and publish it to eMASS. It describes the boundary, the components, the information types and categorization, and how each SADD outcome is implemented. The AO approves an initial SSP before the interim authorization to test, and the full SSP with the authorization package. It is reviewed each year and with each continuous monitoring cycle.

## PL-4

Every user will sign the rules of behavior before their account is created, and each year after. A lapsed acknowledgment disables the account, and the ISSM refers violations to the PSO.

## PL-4(1)

The rules of behavior will restrict what users may post about KESTREL on social media and external sites, and what external applications they may use with KESTREL information.

## PL-8

KESTREL's security architecture will be the SADD, built from the CCSD and the SABER. The AO approves it as the design basis, and it is rebuilt whenever the CCSD, the system description, or the SABER version changes.

## PL-9

Security controls that apply everywhere will be managed centrally. Policy as code is defined once, in GitLab, and deployed to every location by the pipeline.

## PL-10

KESTREL's baseline will be selected from the CNSSI 1253 columns for its categorization of High confidentiality, High integrity, and Moderate availability.

## PL-11

The baseline will be tailored in the CCSD, through tailoring actions the PMO ISSE drafts, the SCA reviews, and the AO approves. Each action records its rationale and the AO's decision.
