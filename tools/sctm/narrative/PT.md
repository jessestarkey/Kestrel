# PT: PII Processing and Transparency

## PT-1

The privacy officer will issue KESTREL's privacy policy and procedures under the Privacy Program Charter and Policy, within the DoD component privacy program. The Configuration Control Board will approve them, and they will be reviewed at least once a year.

## PT-2

KESTREL will process personal information only where its authority allows. The Privacy Impact Assessment, on DD Form 2930, records that authority before user and service desk records and the prior collection imagery arrive. The privacy officer approves each new training set's handling before training starts.

## PT-3

Personal information will be processed only for stated purposes. User and service desk records support account management and support, and incidental imagery of people is kept only where a detection task needs it. No model is trained to identify individuals.

## PT-4

Consent will be handled under the DoD component privacy program, which KESTREL inherits.

## PT-5

The account request form will carry a Privacy Act statement, so each user receives notice when their personal information is collected.

## PT-5(2)

A Privacy Act statement will be given wherever KESTREL collects personal information directly from a person, starting with the account request form.

## PT-6

User and service desk records will be covered by the DoD-wide system of records notices that the component privacy office names, which KESTREL inherits.

## PT-6(1)

The routine uses of those records will be reviewed under the DoD-wide notices, which KESTREL inherits.

## PT-6(2)

Exemption rules for those records will come from the DoD-wide notices, which KESTREL inherits.

## PT-7

The Privacy Impact Assessment will identify any special category of personal information KESTREL processes. KESTREL collects no Social Security numbers, and accounts key on the CAC DoD ID number.

## PT-7(2)

Intelligence oversight rules bar KESTREL from collecting information about how anyone exercises their First Amendment rights. The compliance mapping register records that obligation.
