# PM: Program Management

## PM-4

One POA&M process will cover every finding. Items live in eMASS from Phase 1, and milestones are reviewed each month and reported to the AO each quarter. An ISSO closes an item only after verifying the fix. From Phase 2, one ranking in Jira orders remediation across every team by risk.

## PM-5(1)

The privacy officer will keep an inventory of the personal information KESTREL holds and where each kind is stored. That is user and service desk records, and incidental imagery of people.

## PM-6

Security performance will be measured and reported. The steering committee reviews a security posture dashboard in Splunk each month, and the AI/ML Governance Review Board sends a posture report to it each quarter. The risk register and the evidence repository feed both.

## PM-7

KESTREL's security architecture will be the SADD, which the AO approves as the design basis, and its components and data flows are described in the System Description.

## PM-9

The risk management strategy will be the Risk Management Program Charter. It sets one assessment method based on NIST SP 800-30, the program's risk appetite, and the chain of authority for accepting risk. The AO approves the appetite statement, and the charter is reviewed each year.

## PM-10

The authorization process will run from the interim authorization to test in Phase 1 to the ATO decision on 2028-09-30, managed by the ISSM in eMASS. After that, ongoing authorization keeps it current, with agreed reauthorization triggers and a status report to the AO each quarter.

## PM-11

KESTREL's mission processes will be defined in the business impact analysis, from flying the aircraft to collecting, analyzing, releasing, and retraining, with how long each can be lost. Each AI use case is defined in the inventory and approved by the review board before work starts.

## PM-14

Testing, training, and monitoring will be coordinated across the program. The parent organization's internal review office audits each governing plan at least once every two years, checking that its procedures are actually carried out.

## PM-17

Contractors and other parties that hold KESTREL's CUI on their own systems will meet DFARS 252.204-7012 and NIST SP 800-171, under their contracts. Before any CUI is shared, the supply chain lead confirms the supplier's SPRS score and its CMMC Level 2 status.

## PM-18

The privacy officer will maintain the Privacy Program Charter and Policy, which sets KESTREL's privacy program at the Privacy Overlay's Low tier within the DoD component privacy program.

## PM-19

The privacy officer will lead KESTREL's privacy program, approving the handling of personal information and each new training set.

## PM-22

The quality of personal information will be managed under the privacy program. Users correct the attributes KESTREL holds about them through Jira Service Management, and CAC and partner attributes are corrected at their source.

## PM-28

The program's risk framing will be set in the risk appetite statement. Appetite is lowest where a failure could reach an aircraft or a key, and moderate for analytic availability and development. The program manager drafts the statement and the AO approves it.

## PM-31

The continuous monitoring strategy will be the Continuous Monitoring Plan. An interim plan is approved in Phase 1 for the interim authorization to test, and the full plan in Phase 2. The AO approves it, and it sets what is monitored, how often, and who receives the results.
