# AT: Awareness and Training

## AT-1

The program manager will issue the awareness and training policy as part of KESTREL's system security policy. The ISSM's training procedures will carry it out. The Configuration Control Board will approve both, and each will be reviewed at least once a year.

## AT-2

Every user will complete DoD cyber awareness and CUI training on JKO before their account is created, and each year after. A lapsed course disables the account in Active Directory through Ansible, and the ground control stations' accounts follow at their next sync.

## AT-2(2)

Awareness training will cover insider threat, including how to recognize and report its indicators.

## AT-2(3)

Awareness training will cover social engineering and the mining of information about KESTREL and its people. Phishing exercises come from the parent organization's and the performer's email programs.

## AT-3

Role-based training will add courses for the roles with the most consequence. These are operators, OT maintainers, the staff who receive hardware, incident responders, and everyone with a contingency role. Each course is taken before the role starts and each year after.

## AT-3(5)

Staff who handle personal information will be trained on how to process it, under the privacy program.

## AT-4

Training completion will be recorded per user, and the record drives the account disable when a course lapses. Records are kept with the user's account records.
