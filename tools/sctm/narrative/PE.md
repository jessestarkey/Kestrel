# PE: Physical and Environmental Protection

## PE-2

The PSO will authorize physical access to the data center and the operating sites, and the parent organization's facility will authorize access to the alternate site. From Phase 2, KESTREL's account lifecycle events will drive the PSO's badge provisioning and removal, so a departure removes a badge as well as an account.

## PE-3

The PSO will run the physical access control systems at the data center and each operating site. KESTREL connects them to its identity platform. From Phase 3, Splunk Enterprise Security correlates badge events with sign-ins, and an operator signed in at a station without having badged into that site raises an alert.
