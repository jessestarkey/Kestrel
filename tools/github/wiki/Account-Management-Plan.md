# Account Management Plan (AMP)

The AMP is being drafted from the SABER's [AMP template](https://github.com/jessestarkey/saber-framework/blob/main/templates/AMP-TEMPLATE.md) and KESTREL's [Identity design intents](https://github.com/jessestarkey/Kestrel/blob/main/docs/03-sadd/02-identity.md). It will record KESTREL's identity assurance levels, role bundles, authentication rules, privileged access tiers, break-glass custody, and access review cadence.

Until it is published, the [Identity section of the SADD](https://github.com/jessestarkey/Kestrel/blob/main/docs/03-sadd/02-identity.md) holds the design decisions it will carry.
