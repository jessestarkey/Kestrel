## Design Intent
The ML Team authors KESTREL's AISP, with each mission team writing the sections for its use cases. The PMO ISSE writes the T&E methodology and names a government AI test lead, so certification does not rest on one person. It follows the DoD AI Cybersecurity Risk Management Tailoring Guide the CCSD applies. The ISSM and the AI/ML Governance Review Board review it, and the Configuration Control Board approves it. It is revised each year and whenever a threshold, the component catalog, or a foundation model changes.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
