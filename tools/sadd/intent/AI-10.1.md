## Design Intent
The ML team authors KESTREL's AISP, with each mission team writing the sections for its use cases. The PMO ISSE writes the T&E methodology, since it runs Certification to Field and AI Cyber T&E. The AI/ML Governance Review Board reviews the AISP, and the Configuration Control Board approves it. It is revised each year and whenever a threshold, the component catalog, or a foundation model changes.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
