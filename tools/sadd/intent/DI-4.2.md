## Design Intent
The permitted services, ports, and protocols for each asset type are set in the CMP and enforced twice. Host firewalls enforce them on every KESTREL-owned host, and the hubs' shared rule base enforces them on the network. The scope is every spoke, the data center, the privileged access workstations, and the VDI desktops. Fleet equipment follows its manufacturer's service list.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
