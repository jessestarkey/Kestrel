## Design Intent
The ISSM opens a POA&M item for every weakness KESTREL intends to fix, whether it comes from the SAR, a penetration test, continuous monitoring, or a STIG finding. Each item carries its owner, corrective action, resources, and dated milestones. Milestones are reviewed each month and reported to the AO each quarter. An item closes only when its fix is verified and the evidence is attached.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
