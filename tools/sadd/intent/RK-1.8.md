## Design Intent
The ISSM opens a POA&M item in eMASS for every weakness KESTREL intends to fix. Sources include the SAR, penetration tests, continuous monitoring, STIG findings, AI Cyber T&E, CSSP directives, and inspections. Each item carries its owner, corrective action, resources, and dated milestones. Milestones are reviewed each month and reported to the AO each quarter. An item closes only when an ISSO verifies the fix by the original test, with the evidence filed in the evidence repository and linked.

## External Dependencies
DISA's eMASS, which holds the POA&M.
