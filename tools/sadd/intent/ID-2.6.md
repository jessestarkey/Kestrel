## Design Intent
Re-evaluate every portal, assistant, and administrative session while it runs, and revoke a session when risk becomes severe. Concurrent sessions are limited per account type, tighter for privileged accounts, and exceeding a limit alerts. Device lock applies everywhere except a ground control station whose aircraft is airborne, under CCSD tailoring action 2. Station sessions are not ended for inactivity while their aircraft is airborne, a parameter the AMP records.

## External Dependencies
The IL5 cloud provider's identity service, inherited under its provisional authorization.
