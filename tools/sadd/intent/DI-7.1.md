## Design Intent
The Security Team authors KESTREL's VPMP, since it owns the POA&M and continuous monitoring, with IT Operations writing the patch windows and Fleet Operations the fleet procedures. The Configuration Control Board approves the VPMP, and risk acceptances under it go to the AO. It is revised each year and whenever an exception such as CCSD tailoring action 3 changes.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
