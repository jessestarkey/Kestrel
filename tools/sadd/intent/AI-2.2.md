## Design Intent
Labels come from three named sources, never from an outside labeling service. These are the Data Team's own annotators, analyst corrections, and model corrections released from the IL6 enclave over F13. A sample of every batch is labeled twice, and low agreement holds the batch. Each correction is bound in its provenance record to its source, either the analyst who made it or its IL6 release record. One source's corrections can therefore be reviewed or withdrawn together.

## External Dependencies
The IL6 enclave's owner, whose release review approves the model corrections it sends.
