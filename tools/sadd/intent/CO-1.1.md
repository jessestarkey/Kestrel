## Design Intent
The PMO ISSE inventories what binds KESTREL and on what authority. That includes DoDI 8510.01, CNSSI 1253, the Cloud Computing SRG at IL5, and the CUI program under DoDI 5200.48. It also includes intelligence oversight under EO 12333 and DoDM 5240.01, the Privacy Act and DoDI 5400.11, and DoDI 5400.16 for PIAs. It covers DoDI 8530.01, DoDM 8140.03, OMB M-21-31, and DFARS 252.204-7012 for the performer. Each is mapped to the outcomes that satisfy it, gaps go to the requirement register, and unowned gaps to the POA&M. The watch runs with the AI regulatory mapping (`GV-4.5`) and is checked each quarter. It also lists airworthiness, Section 508, and foreign disclosure, tracked outside the SADD.

## External Dependencies
DoD, OMB, and the other issuers whose requirements bind KESTREL.
