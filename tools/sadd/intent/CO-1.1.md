## Design Intent
The PMO ISSE inventories what binds KESTREL and on what authority. That includes DoDI 8510.01 and the Cloud Computing SRG at IL5, and the CUI program under DoDI 5200.48. It also includes DFARS 252.204-7012 and the CMMC clauses for the performer, the Privacy Act and DoDI 5400.11, and the foreign disclosure rules governing partner releases. Each is broken into requirements and mapped to the outcomes that satisfy it, and gaps go to the gap register. The inventory is checked each quarter for new or revised issuances.

## External Dependencies
DoD, OMB, and the other issuers whose requirements bind KESTREL.
