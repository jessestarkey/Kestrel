## Design Intent
A risk owner proposes an acceptance through the service desk, and the ISSM scores it against the tolerance bands, which route it to the right authority. Every acceptance records its rationale, compensating measures, and an expiry no later than one year. CCSD tailoring action 3, which expires on 2027-09-30, is the model. Acceptances are re-reviewed before they expire, and an expired acceptance becomes an open finding.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
