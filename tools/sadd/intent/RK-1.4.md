## Design Intent
A risk owner proposes an acceptance through the service desk. The ISSM scores it against the tolerance bands. A Low or Very Low risk goes to the PMO ISSE under the AO's delegation, and everything else goes to the AO. A risk above Very Low that touches CCSD rows 6, 8, 10, or 13 always goes to the AO. The accepting authority is never the team whose delivery the finding blocks. Every acceptance records its rationale, compensating measures, and an expiry no later than one year. Acceptances are re-reviewed each quarter and before they expire, and a second extension goes to the AO. An expired acceptance becomes an open finding.

## External Dependencies
DISA's eMASS, where accepted risks are recorded.
