## Design Intent
KESTREL's internet-reachable endpoints fall under the DoD Vulnerability Disclosure Program, which sets what researchers may test. The PMO ISSE confirms with DC3 that the CAC-gated endpoints behind the access point are in scope. Reports it relays reach the Security Team, which triages them and tracks each one to closure like a penetration test finding.

## External Dependencies
The DoD Cyber Crime Center, which runs the DoD Vulnerability Disclosure Program.
