## Design Intent
Every red team finding above the AISP's must-fix severity needs a validated fix, or a risk acceptance, before the use case can be certified. The PMO ISSE signs off on each closure. Any acceptance routes under Risk Acceptance Process (`RK-1.4`), and anything above Very Low goes to the AO, since every certification touches CCSD row 8 or row 10.

## External Dependencies
The penetration testing provider, whose AI red team runs each exercise under its rules of engagement.
