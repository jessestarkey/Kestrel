## Design Intent
One program evidence repository receives the pipeline's evidence store, continuous monitoring results, and each team's process records. The repository is append-only for artifacts, with access limited by role. Fleet and OT evidence is entered by hand after each maintenance window. Each artifact is tagged to its control and outcome and held for its records schedule, at least one year after the system is retired. Before each assessment cycle, the ISSOs check the repository for gaps and stale artifacts, and a compliance posture dashboard shows its state.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
