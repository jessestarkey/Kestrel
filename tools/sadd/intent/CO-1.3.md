## Design Intent
One program evidence repository receives the pipeline's evidence store, continuous monitoring results, and each team's process records. Each artifact is tagged to its control and outcome. Artifacts are held for their record class's retention, and authorization evidence is held for the life of the authorization. Before each assessment cycle, the ISSOs check the repository for gaps and stale artifacts, and a compliance posture dashboard shows its state.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
