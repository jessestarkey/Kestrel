## Design Intent
The privacy officer is accountable for KESTREL's privacy program under DoDI 5400.11. The personal information it holds is user account records, service desk records, and incidental imagery of people in the collection. The privacy policy covers how each is collected, used, kept, and disposed of. Users can request correction of their own account records through the service desk. The program is reviewed each year.

## External Dependencies
The DoD component's privacy office, which oversees the privacy program.
