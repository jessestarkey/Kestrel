## Design Intent
The privacy officer is accountable for KESTREL's privacy program under DoDI 5400.11, at the Privacy Overlay's Low tier. The personal information it holds is user account records, service desk records, partner credential records, user identifiers in security telemetry, and incidental imagery of people. The privacy policy covers how each is collected, used, kept, and disposed of. Analysts handling raw imagery, Data Team stewards, and service desk staff take role-based privacy training. Users correct KESTREL-held attributes through the service desk, while CAC and partner attributes are corrected at their source. The program is reviewed each year.

## External Dependencies
The DoD component's privacy office, which oversees the privacy program.
