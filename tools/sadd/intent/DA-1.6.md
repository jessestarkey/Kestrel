## Design Intent
Report documents released to partner nations carry rights protection from KESTREL's Microsoft 365 DoD tenant. It limits each document to the receiving nation and expires it on the foreign disclosure officer's terms. Wrapped reports travel through the partner API, and each partner's cross-cloud configuration is a term of its memorandum of agreement. Machine-readable detections are not wrapped, since partner systems consume them directly under the release review and the memoranda.

## External Dependencies
KESTREL's Microsoft 365 DoD tenant, inherited at IL5. Each partner nation, whose systems must honor the rights protection under its memorandum of agreement.
