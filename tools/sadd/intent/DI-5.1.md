## Design Intent
Application allow-listing runs deny-by-default on the privileged access workstations, the VDI desktops, and every data center and cloud virtual machine. The privileged access workstations accept no removable media, and the VDI desktops already block drive and USB redirection. Cameras and microphones are disabled on the privileged access workstations and in the VDI image.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
