## Design Intent
One ranking orders remediation across every team. It combines the risk score, the asset's mission criticality, known exploitation, and exposure, so a known exploited flaw on a DMZ-facing or fleet asset outranks everything else. The team leads and the ISSM agree priorities each week, and the PMO ISSE recalibrates the model each quarter against what was actually exploited.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
