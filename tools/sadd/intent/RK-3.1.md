## Design Intent
One ranking orders remediation across every team. It combines the risk score, the asset's mission criticality, known exploitation, exposure, and effort, so a known exploited flaw on a DMZ, Tier 0, or fleet asset outranks everything else. Deadlines from IAVM directives and CYBERCOM orders are fixed inputs, and OT maintenance windows are a constraint. The team leads and the ISSM agree priorities each week and record any deviation from the ranking. The PMO ISSE recalibrates the model each quarter against the deviation log and what was actually exploited.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
