## Design Intent
Analysts investigate in the security monitoring platform's workbench, which holds every telemetry source in one place. Related alerts are folded into one incident, and each investigation's queries and findings are saved with the incident record. When an investigation needs more detail, the owning team raises logging on the affected components within one hour of the request and returns it to baseline at closure. On fleet equipment this goes only through the vendor's engineering path, with Fleet Operations' concurrence.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
