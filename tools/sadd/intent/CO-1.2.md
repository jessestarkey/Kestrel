## Design Intent
Scheduled scans evaluate every surface against its baseline. Authenticated scans with DISA SCAP content cover hosts, configuration checks cover the cloud, benchmark checks cover containers, and manufacturer baselines cover fleet equipment. Their machine-readable results feed one compliance status view. Drift from a baseline alerts the owning team, and each finding is tracked to closure under the VPMP's deadlines.

## External Dependencies
DISA, which publishes the STIGs and SCAP content the scans use.
