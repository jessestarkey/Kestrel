## Design Intent
Continuous monitoring results feed the ISSM's view of KESTREL's authorization posture. The triggers for AO review are agreed in advance. They include a new interconnection, partner nation, spoke, payload vendor, or foundation model, and any change to the CCSD's information types. Activation of the alternate site is another. So are a slip of the replacement payload processing workstation past 2027-09-30, which needs the AO to re-date CCSD tailoring action 3, and tailoring action 6's annual review. The ISSM reports authorization status to the AO each quarter.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
