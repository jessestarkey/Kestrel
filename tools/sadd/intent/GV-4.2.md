## Design Intent
The PMO ISSE maps KESTREL's whole AI estate as one architecture each year, covering its trust zones, external interfaces, and the paths between models, use cases, and agents. The PMO ISSE then ranks the MITRE ATLAS techniques that matter most across it. The assessment goes to the AI/ML Governance Review Board and the ML and mission teams, and it feeds each model's threat model.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
