## Design Intent
Every supplier answers a security questionnaire before contract award. Suppliers are tiered by what they can reach. Tier 1 covers anyone touching the fleet, mission data, or keys, such as the cloud provider, the aircraft manufacturer, and the payload vendors. Tier 1 suppliers are reassessed each year and the rest every three years. Findings are tracked to closure. Every self-hosted third-party product is registered in the asset inventory once its assessment passes.

## External Dependencies
The payload vendors, the aircraft and ground equipment manufacturers, the service desk vendor, the cloud provider, the CSSP, and the other suppliers in the vendor population.
