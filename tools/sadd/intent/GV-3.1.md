## Design Intent
Commercial suppliers answer a security questionnaire before contract award, including the Phase 1 awards. DoD and provider services, such as the IL5 cloud provider and the CSSP, are relied on through their authorizations under Inherited Control Validation (`GV-5.12`) instead. Suppliers are tiered by what they can reach. Tier 1 covers anyone touching the fleet, mission data, or keys, and is reassessed each year, the rest every three years. Findings are tracked to closure, and one a vendor will not close escalates to the steering committee. Every self-hosted third-party product is registered in the asset inventory once its assessment passes.

## External Dependencies
The payload vendors, the aircraft and ground equipment manufacturers, the data center hardware and HSM vendors, the service desk vendor, and the other commercial suppliers.
