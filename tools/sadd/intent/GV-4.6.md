## Design Intent
The board receives each AI supplier risk finding and decides what to do about it. Findings come from model selection, label integrity, foundation model dependency governance, and vendor assessment. Each AI supplier is tiered, and acceptances of supplier risk are logged with the AO's visibility. Each foundation model and other vendor-delivered model is registered in the inventory once its risk is accepted.

## External Dependencies
The cloud provider, the foundation model's publisher, and the publishers of pretrained checkpoints.
