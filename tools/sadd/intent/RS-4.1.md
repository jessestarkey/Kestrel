## Design Intent
The ISSM authors KESTREL's IRP. An interim IRP covers the interim authorization to test from the first build, until the full plan and its runbooks are approved. Fleet Operations writes the OT containment rules, the ML Team writes the AI runbooks, and the CSSP confirms the responsibility split. The Configuration Control Board approves the IRP, and it is revised each year and after every exercise or major incident that changes it.

## External Dependencies
The CSSP, under the detection and response split in its service agreement.
