## Design Intent
Write one AMP covering every KESTREL identity population, including partners, AI agents, and OT devices. It records the assurance levels, the role bundles, the maintenance-window rules, and the break-glass procedures. It also records the station exceptions: session lock under CCSD tailoring action 2, no lockout or idle logout while an aircraft is airborne, and the revocation grace period.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
