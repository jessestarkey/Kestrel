## Design Intent
Security logs stay searchable for 12 months and then move to the archive for 18 more, following OMB M-21-31. Alert and investigation records are kept for as long as their case or POA&M item requires, if longer. All security telemetry is also forwarded to the CSSP, which keeps its own copy under its service agreement.

## External Dependencies
The CSSP, which receives forwarded telemetry and adds its own detection under its service agreement.
