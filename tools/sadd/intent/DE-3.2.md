## Design Intent
Security logs stay searchable for 12 months and then move to the archive for 18 more. That is KESTREL's AU-11 parameter, with OMB M-21-31 adopted as the benchmark. Alert and investigation records are kept for as long as their case or POA&M item requires, if longer. Archived logs are restored and read each year to prove they can be. All security telemetry is also forwarded to the CSSP, which keeps its own copy under its service agreement.

## External Dependencies
The CSSP, which receives forwarded telemetry and adds its own detection under its service agreement.
