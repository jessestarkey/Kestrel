## Design Intent
The program manager chairs a monthly security steering committee of the chief engineer, the PMO ISSE, the ISSM, the team leads, and the intelligence mission leads. It settles cross-team disputes, risk questions above the Configuration Control Board, and schedule conflicts with security work. Its decisions and actions are tracked in the service desk, and a leadership dashboard shows the program's security posture each month.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
