## Design Intent
The SCA authors the Security Assessment Plan for each assessment cycle. It covers the cloud, the data center, the alternate site, the fleet through spares and the simulator, and the AI models through their evidence annexes and testing in stage. Controls inherited from each provider under Inherited Control Validation (`GV-5.12`) are assessed by reviewing their packages. The ISSM and the PMO ISSE review its scope against the SCTM, and the AO approves it. It is revised each cycle and whenever the boundary changes.

## External Dependencies
The SCA, who assesses KESTREL independently for the AO. DISA's eMASS, which holds the plan.
