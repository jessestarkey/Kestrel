## Design Intent
The ISSM maintains the SCTM in OSCAL. It has a row for every control in the CNSSI 1253 selection, its overlays, and the tailoring actions. Each row carries the control to its SADD outcome, its implementation in the SSP, its inheritance status, and its evidence. This SADD's coverage table fills only the outcome column, and every unclaimed row becomes a gap register entry. It is validated against the SSP before each submission and updated with every change.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
