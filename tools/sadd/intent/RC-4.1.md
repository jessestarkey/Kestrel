## Design Intent
The Platform Team authors KESTREL's CP, with Fleet Operations writing the fleet sections and each team writing its restoration order. It defines the known state each tier is restored to and the checks a restored system passes before returning to service. It names the critical assets from the business impact analysis and plans for the data center never returning. It names the recovery coordinator, the restore team, the alternate site and carrier liaisons, and the operations lead as the fleet's operations authority. Each has a backup, with contacts reachable without the identity provider. The ISSM reviews it, and the Configuration Control Board approves it. Anything shared with partners goes through the foreign disclosure officer, and activating the alternate site is an AO review trigger. It is revised each year and after every exercise.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
