## Design Intent
The third-party penetration testers test all of KESTREL at least once a year and the paths reachable from outside every six months. They also test after any major change, such as a new spoke, interconnection, or external API. Fleet systems are tested on spares or a simulator. Each finding becomes a POA&M item and is retested before it closes. The testers act as an independent check on the design the PMO ISSE produced.

## External Dependencies
The penetration testing provider, under each engagement's rules of engagement.
