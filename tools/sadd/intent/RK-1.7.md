## Design Intent
The SCA writes the RAR. It reads the SAR's findings, the open risk register, and the current threat picture together to show what they mean for KESTREL's mission. It is reviewed with the ISSM, the PMO ISSE, and the risk owners and delivered to the AO alongside the SAR. The SCA reissues it when a reauthorization trigger fires or the threat brief reports a material change.

## External Dependencies
The SCA, who assesses KESTREL independently for the AO. DISA's eMASS, which holds the RAR.
