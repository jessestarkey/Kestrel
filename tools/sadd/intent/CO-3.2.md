## Design Intent
The program manager receives each external audit and inspection request, and the ISSM assembles the evidence. These include inspectors general, GAO, and JFHQ-DODIN's Command Cyber Operational Readiness Inspections. DCMA and DIBCAC reviews cover the performer's CMMC laptops that KESTREL relies on. Each request's authority, scope, and deadline are confirmed. Evidence is packaged from the program evidence repository and reviewed for CUI marking and releasability before it leaves the program. Findings are tracked to closure in the POA&M.

## External Dependencies
Inspectors general, GAO, JFHQ-DODIN, DCMA, and DIBCAC, whose audits and inspections KESTREL responds to.
