## Design Intent
The ISSM is the single intake point for external audits and inspections. These include inspectors general, GAO, cyber readiness inspections, and DCMA or DIBCAC reviews of the performer. The ISSM confirms each request's authority, scope, and deadline. Evidence is packaged from the program evidence repository rather than assembled anew, and findings are tracked to closure in the POA&M.

## External Dependencies
Inspectors general, GAO, DoD cyber readiness inspectors, DCMA, and DIBCAC, whose audits and inspections KESTREL responds to.
