## Design Intent
The PMO ISSE authors this SADD from the AO-approved CCSD, generating its carried fields from the SABER and writing its design intents by hand. The chief engineer and the ISSM review it, and the AO accepts it as KESTREL's design basis. It is rebuilt whenever the CCSD, the system description, or the SABER version changes.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
