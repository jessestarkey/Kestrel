## Design Intent
The PMO ISSE authors this SADD from the AO-approved CCSD, generating its carried fields from the SABER and writing its design intents by hand. The chief engineer and the ISSM review it, and the AO approves it as KESTREL's design basis. Its approval depends on the AO approving CCSD version 1.1, whose changes it already designs. It is reviewed each year. It is rebuilt whenever a CCSD revision changes the approved control set, or the system description or the SABER version changes.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
