## Design Intent
Behavioral baselines cover every person role, including analysts, administrators, data scientists, developers, and partner accounts. They also cover pipeline, workload, and AI agent identities. Bulk reads, access outside normal hours, and reads of another team's or vendor's data raise a risk score. The PSO, program counsel, and the privacy officer set the monitored population, retention, and who may see a score before profiling starts, and thresholds are recorded in the CMP. The Security Team investigates high scores and refers insider threat indicators to the PSO's program.

## External Dependencies
The PSO's insider threat program, which receives referrals and owns every case.
