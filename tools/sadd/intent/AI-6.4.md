## Design Intent
The PMO ISSE decides pass or fail at the gate. Each proposed acceptance is scored and routed under Risk Acceptance Process (`RK-1.4`). Every gate acceptance touches CCSD row 8 or row 10, so anything above Very Low goes to the AO. Every acceptance is logged with its rationale, approver, compensating measures, and expiry, and feeds the program's risk register.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
