## Design Intent
Each model endpoint can cut back to its previous certified version in minutes. The owning team operates each AI agent's and language model use case's kill switch directly. The Security Team and the CSSP trigger it only through the pre-approved containment playbook that suspends a workload or AI agent identity. An agent that misbehaves short of a kill is first limited to read-only drafting, then suspended, and its session record is kept as evidence. Each aircraft keeps its prior certified edge model, and the operator can turn onboard detection off.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
