## Design Intent
Each quarter, the Platform Team restores a sample from every tier using only the written procedure, and measures the result against the tier's objectives. The sample rotates through exported services and fleet captures, which Fleet Operations restores to spares or the simulator. A full restore of the identity provider and of one mission team's prod spoke runs once a year. The registry is restored and every model, signed release, and baseline is verified against its signature, protecting CCSD rows 8 and 10.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
