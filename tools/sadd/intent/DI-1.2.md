## Design Intent
Passive sensors on the ground segment network fingerprint the ground control stations, launch and recovery equipment, and operating site services from their own traffic. They parse the UAS command and telemetry protocols, and maintenance records fill what they cannot see. They never probe a device, so discovery cannot disturb a station during a flight. Sensors sit at each operating site and are checked for coverage after each change. Aircraft are inventoried by tail number and reconciled each time one connects. Fleet records reach the inventory over F22.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
