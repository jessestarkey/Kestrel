## Design Intent
KESTREL's responsible AI policy adopts the DoD AI ethical principles, which call for AI that is responsible, equitable, traceable, reliable, and governable. It commits that no model commands an aircraft, that a person approves every AI agent action affecting a flight, and that analysts can see when AI contributed to a product. Each principle maps to the AI outcomes that enforce it, and the policy is part of every user's training.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
