## Design Intent
Every user acknowledges KESTREL's rules of behavior before an account is issued, and again each year. Privileged users sign additional privileged rules, and partner users sign the partner rules their nation's agreement requires. The identity provider records each acknowledgment, and a missing or expired one suspends the account. Violations go to the ISSM and, where they suggest insider risk, to the PSO.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
