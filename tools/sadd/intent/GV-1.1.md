## Design Intent
KESTREL's policy tier is one system security policy that the program manager issues beneath DoD issuances and the parent organization's enterprise policy. Its standards are the governing plans this SADD names, namely the AMP, CMP, VPMP, ASP, AISP, DMP, KMP, IRP, CP, SCRM plan, ConMon plan, privacy policy, and responsible AI policy. Its procedures are the runbooks beneath them. Each policy statement maps to the control families it satisfies. The policy covers everything inside the boundary, naming the UAS fleet and ground segment, the alternate site, and the performer laptops that hold source code.

## External Dependencies
The parent organization, whose enterprise security policy KESTREL's policy sits beneath.
