## Design Intent
KESTREL's policy tier is one system security policy that the program manager issues beneath DoD issuances and the parent organization's enterprise policy. Its standards are the governing plans this SADD names, namely the AMP, CMP, VPMP, ASP, AISP, DMP, KMP, IRP, CP, and SCRM. Its procedures are the runbooks beneath them. Each policy statement maps to the control families it satisfies, and the policy applies to everyone and everything inside the boundary.

## External Dependencies
The parent organization, whose enterprise security policy KESTREL's policy sits beneath.
