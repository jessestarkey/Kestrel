## Design Intent
Admit coalition partners on External Certificate Authority or federated partner PKI credentials, each under a named KESTREL sponsor. Partner identities map only to partner API roles, and every external credential is revocation-checked at each session. The trust list names each of the five partner nations' issuing authorities, and adding a partner is a CCSD revision trigger.

## External Dependencies
DoD PKI, External Certificate Authorities, and each partner nation's PKI or identity provider, under the memoranda of agreement listed in the system description.
