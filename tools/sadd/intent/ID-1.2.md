## Design Intent
Admit vendor support engineers on External Certificate Authority credentials from the first build, and coalition partners on ECA or federated partner PKI credentials once they onboard, each under a named KESTREL sponsor. Partner identities map only to partner API roles and vendor identities only to their support cases, and every external credential is revocation-checked at each session. The trust list names each partner nation's issuing authorities, and adding a partner nation is a CCSD revision trigger.

## External Dependencies
DoD PKI, External Certificate Authorities, and each partner nation's PKI or identity provider, under the memoranda of agreement listed in the system description.
