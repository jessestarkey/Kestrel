## Design Intent
Admit vendor support engineers on External Certificate Authority credentials at the Medium Hardware level from the first build. Coalition partner systems connect on their nation's PKI or ECA certificates once they onboard, each under a named KESTREL sponsor. Partner identities map only to partner API roles and vendor identities only to their support cases. Every external credential is revocation-checked at each session. The trust list names each partner nation's issuing authorities, and adding a partner nation is a CCSD revision trigger.

## External Dependencies
DoD PKI, External Certificate Authorities, and each partner nation's PKI or identity provider, under the memoranda of agreement listed in the system description.
