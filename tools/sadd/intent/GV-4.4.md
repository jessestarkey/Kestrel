## Design Intent
The chief engineer chairs the AI/ML Governance Review Board. Its members are the PMO ISSE, the privacy officer, program legal counsel, the ML team lead, and the sponsoring intelligence mission lead, with the foreign disclosure officer joining when partner release is involved. The board decides each use case within ten business days by approving it, approving it with conditions, or rejecting it, against the responsible AI policy and the use case's risk tier. Decisions and conditions are tracked to closure, and a posture report goes to the steering committee each quarter.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
