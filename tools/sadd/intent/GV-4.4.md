## Design Intent
The chief engineer chairs the AI/ML Governance Review Board, chartered by the program office. Its members are the PMO ISSE, the privacy officer, program legal counsel, the ML Team lead, and the sponsoring intelligence mission lead, with the foreign disclosure officer joining for partner release. The lowest risk tier gets a desk review by the PMO ISSE. The board decides other use cases within ten business days by approving, approving with conditions, or rejecting them. Until the responsible AI policy is issued, it judges against the AISP. Disputed decisions escalate to the steering committee, and a posture report goes there each quarter.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
