## Design Intent
The program manager drafts KESTREL's risk appetite statement, and the AO approves it. Appetite is lowest for flight safety, the command link, key material, and the High-integrity models and releases (CCSD rows 6, 8, 10, and 13). Any residual risk above Very Low there goes to the AO. Elsewhere it is moderate for analytic availability and for development environments. The ISSM and ISSOs score each request and recommend. The AO accepts every risk, and a standing AO delegation recorded in the authorization decision lets the PMO ISSE accept Low ones. If the PMO ISSE is unavailable, the request waits for the AO. The statement is reviewed each year with the steering committee.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
