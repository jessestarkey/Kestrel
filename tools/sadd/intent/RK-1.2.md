## Design Intent
KESTREL's appetite is lowest for risk to flight safety, the command link, and key material, where only Very Low residual risk is tolerated without AO action. It is moderate for analytic availability and for development environments. Each tolerance band maps to an acceptance authority. The ISSM may accept Low, the program manager Moderate, and only the AO High or Very High. The statement is reviewed each year with the steering committee.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
