## Design Intent
One approved AI component catalog lists the model families, pretrained checkpoints, datasets, guardrail models, frameworks, and model registries KESTREL may use. The ML Team lead owns it, and the AI/ML Governance Review Board approves additions. It is reviewed each year and on any red team finding, CVE, or supply chain incident that touches a listed item. The ML Team lead can suspend an item at once, and the ISSO confirms the suspension within 24 hours. The package mirror and the model registry accept only what the catalog lists.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
