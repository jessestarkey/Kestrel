## Design Intent
KESTREL releases no datasets outside the program today. Partner API products and IL6 baselines are released under their own reviews, not as datasets. A future dataset release needs de-identification of incidental imagery of people, a re-identification test, and approval from the privacy officer and the foreign disclosure officer. The privacy officer's approved de-identification techniques already apply to training and test data. The PMO ISSE is the only designated poster of public content, which is limited to the vulnerability disclosure listing. Anything posted passes OPSEC and foreign disclosure review first and is rechecked each year.

## External Dependencies
The DoD Vulnerability Disclosure Program, which publishes the listing.
