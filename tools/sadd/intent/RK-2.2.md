## Design Intent
The Security Team's threat intelligence analyst tracks the actors and campaigns likely to target KESTREL's mission and the techniques they are adopting. These include datalink jamming and spoofing and MITRE ATLAS techniques against models. Each new technique is mapped against the detections already built, and any that would succeed becomes a finding. A monthly threat brief goes to the steering committee and the risk register, and urgent changes go to the ISSM at once.

## External Dependencies
The CSSP and DoD intelligence producers.
