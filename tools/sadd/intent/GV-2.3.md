## Design Intent
Every person with access completes DoD cyber awareness and CUI training before getting an account, and each year after. Role-based training covers privileged users, security roles, developers, UAS operators, OT maintainers, the ML and Data Teams, and receiving staff, who learn to spot counterfeit parts. Privileged users and security roles also meet DoDM 8140.03 qualification for their work roles. The parent organization's and performer's email systems run phishing exercises, inherited, and their results come back to target follow-up training. KESTREL tests IT Operations against pretexted account-reset requests. The identity provider suspends any account whose training has lapsed.

## External Dependencies
The parent organization and the performer, whose enterprise email systems run the phishing exercises.
