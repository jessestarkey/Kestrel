## Design Intent
The cloud hub's next-generation firewall sits at the center of the ten spokes, each peered only to the hub, and runs as a highly available pair. Its rules deny traffic from any dev/test spoke to any prod spoke, apart from F25's shared services, and between the two mission teams' spokes. Every site perimeter is a redundant pair. Outbound traffic is limited to an allow-list of named classes over F29 and F30. These are package sources through the mirror, PKI revocation, the time reference, the threat feed, the Microsoft 365 DoD tenant, and the paging service. The CSSP's feeds use F11 and F31. Both hubs inspect outbound TLS, and east-west mutual TLS between workload identities is exempt from decryption.

## External Dependencies
The IL5 cloud provider, whose controls KESTREL inherits under its provisional authorization. The provider supplies the virtual machines and networking the cloud hub's firewalls run on. KESTREL's Microsoft 365 DoD tenant, an allow-listed destination.
