## Design Intent
The PMO ISSE defines one semi-quantitative method based on NIST SP 800-30, scoring likelihood and impact on five-point scales and combining them in a matrix. Impact is anchored to the CCSD's categorization and to mission effects. Anything that could cost an aircraft, compromise the command link, or expose CCSD row 13's key material scores at the top. The Security Team and the team leads are trained on the method, and the PMO ISSE reviews it each year.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
