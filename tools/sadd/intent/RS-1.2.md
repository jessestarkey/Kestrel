## Design Intent
The ISSM leads every incident, with the ISSOs and security analysts as the hands-on investigators. The CSSP contains what cannot wait for the on-call analyst, using the pre-approved playbooks. The Platform Team, IT Operations, the Data Team, and Fleet Operations act on their own systems at the ISSM's direction. For a model incident, the ML Team owns the model and the mission team owns the use case. Every responder trains on KESTREL's own tools and runbooks before joining the on-call rotation and each year after, with simulated incidents in the exercise environment. The program manager funds the CSSP and tooling through the program budget.

## External Dependencies
The CSSP, under the detection and response split in its service agreement.
