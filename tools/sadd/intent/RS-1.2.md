## Design Intent
The ISSM leads every incident, with the ISSOs and security analysts as the hands-on responders. The Platform Team, IT Operations, and Fleet Operations act on their own systems at the ISSM's direction. For a model incident, the ML team owns the model and the mission team owns the use case, and both join the response. Every responder trains on KESTREL's own tools and runbooks each year, and the AO funds the CSSP and tooling through the program budget.

## External Dependencies
The CSSP, under the detection and response split in its service agreement.
