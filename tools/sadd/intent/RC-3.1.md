## Design Intent
Tier 1 has a recovery time of 4 hours and a recovery point of 15 minutes. Tier 2 has 24 hours for both. Tier 3 has a recovery time of 7 days and a recovery point of 24 hours. Raw collection has an extra safety net, since aircraft storage modules keep each recording until the data lake confirms it. These objectives are checked against every recovery test, and any tier that misses is raised to the AO.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
