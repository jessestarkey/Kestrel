## Design Intent
Tier 1 has a recovery time of 4 hours and a recovery point of 15 minutes. Tier 2 has 24 hours for both. Tier 3 has a recovery time of 7 days and a recovery point of 24 hours. Raw collection has an extra safety net, since aircraft storage modules keep each recording until the backup holding it completes. The objectives come from the business impact analysis (`RK-3.2`), and the program manager approves them. Every recovery test is checked against them, and any tier that misses is raised to the AO.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
