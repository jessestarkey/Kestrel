## Design Intent
The chief engineer chairs the Configuration Control Board. Its members are the PMO ISSE, the ISSM, the Platform Team lead, Fleet Operations' operations lead, and the lead of any team whose change is on the agenda. It meets weekly, with an out-of-cycle emergency path whose decisions get the retrospective review in Asset Lifecycle Change Control (`DI-2.2`). It approves changes to the hubs' rule base, the identity provider, interconnections, system prompts' roles and limits, and every governing plan the AO does not approve. It sends significant changes through Security Impact Analysis, and its decisions are logged with their rationale.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
