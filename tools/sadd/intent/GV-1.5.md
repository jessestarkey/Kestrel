## Design Intent
The chief engineer chairs the Configuration Control Board. Its members are the PMO ISSE, the ISSM, the Platform Team lead, Fleet Operations' operations lead, and the lead of any team whose change is on the agenda. It meets weekly. It approves changes to the hubs' rule base, the identity provider, interconnections, and every governing plan, and it sends significant changes through Security Impact Analysis. Its decisions are logged with their rationale.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
