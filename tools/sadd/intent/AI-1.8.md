## Design Intent
No KESTREL model may command an aircraft, its flight controls, or its payload. Onboard detection models only cue the operator, and every AI agent output that would affect a flight, such as the mission-planning agent's drafts, takes effect only after an operator approves it. The AI/ML Governance Review Board enforces this list at intake, and agent tool scoping enforces it technically. Airworthiness certification remains a separate process.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
