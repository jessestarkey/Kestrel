## Design Intent
Data center and alternate site hardware maintenance is scheduled through the service desk, escorted, and recorded, and failed drives stay on site for sanitization. Fleet Operations maintains the ground control stations and launch equipment only in scheduled windows, with vendor sessions brokered and recorded under Access Process for Third-Party Support (`ID-4.7`). Diagnostic tools and media are approved and scanned before use. Spare stations and aircraft parts cover the mission's tolerance, and they double as the test articles for penetration testing.

## External Dependencies
The IL5 cloud provider, whose controls KESTREL inherits under its provisional authorization. The provider maintains the cloud hardware. The aircraft and ground equipment manufacturers provide support under their maintenance agreements.
