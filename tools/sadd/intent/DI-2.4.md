## Design Intent
On-premises hardware maintenance is scheduled through the service desk, escorted, and recorded, and failed drives stay on site for sanitization. Fleet Operations maintains fleet equipment only in scheduled windows, with vendor sessions brokered under Access Process for Third-Party Support (`ID-4.7`). Diagnostic tools and media are approved and scanned before use. Spares cover the mission's tolerance, and the HSMs, identity servers, and hub firewalls have support agreements with replacement times. A spare used for penetration testing is reimaged and re-verified against its baseline before it returns to the pool.

## External Dependencies
The IL5 cloud provider, which maintains the cloud hardware under its provisional authorization. The aircraft and ground equipment manufacturers, under their maintenance agreements. The parent organization's facility and the PSO, for escorted maintenance at the alternate site.
