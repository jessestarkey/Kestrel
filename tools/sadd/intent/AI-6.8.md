## Design Intent
Edge models are quantized or pruned versions of the cloud detection models. Each optimized artifact is certified as its own version through full Certification to Field and re-tested for accuracy and robustness, never through the envelope fast path. The AISP lists the approved optimization methods, and an unlisted one needs the ISSO's and the review board's approval. Each optimization is recorded against the model in the registry.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
