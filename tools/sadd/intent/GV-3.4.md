## Design Intent
Aircraft, stations, and payloads are bought only from their manufacturers, and data center hardware and HSMs only from authorized resellers. Everything is inspected for tampering and counterfeit parts at receipt. Firmware is accepted only with a valid manufacturer signature. Retired components are sanitized, and the sanitization is verified and recorded before disposal.

## External Dependencies
The aircraft, ground equipment, and data center hardware manufacturers and their authorized resellers.
