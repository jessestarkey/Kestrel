## Design Intent
Removable media is prohibited except for the aircraft's mission storage modules, which are marked CUI, logged when they leave an aircraft, and transported in controlled containers. Failed drives and retired storage modules are sanitized on site before disposal. KESTREL holds no classified data, so no media is downgraded, and data moves to and from the IL6 enclave only through the cross domain solution.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
