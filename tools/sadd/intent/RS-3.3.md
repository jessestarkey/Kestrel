## Design Intent
No containment action may touch a ground control station, the launch and recovery equipment, or a datalink while an aircraft under that station's control is airborne. Such actions wait until Fleet Operations' operations lead approves them or the aircraft has landed. Cutting the ground segment conduit is pre-approved, since it carries only file transfers and cannot affect a flight. The manual runbook covers safe recovery of airborne aircraft, either by handing them to another station or recovering them, before a station is isolated.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
