## Design Intent
Approve each vendor support session per support case, broker and record it through the privileged access platform, and end it when the case closes. Vendors connect over F37 only from the vendor host pool in the VDI spoke, using ECA Medium Hardware credentials. Ground control station and payload vendors work only within maintenance windows, either on site under escort or through that brokered path.

## External Dependencies
Ground control station, payload, and launch equipment vendors, under their support agreements.
