## Design Intent
The roles are the AO and the program manager for the government, and the PMO ISSE, who owns the security design and Certification to Field. The performer ISSM owns the authorization package, and the ISSOs own day-to-day compliance. The SCA assesses, the PSO owns personnel and physical security, and each team lead owns their team's systems. A responsibility matrix in the SSP records who decides what. It is reviewed each year and on any change of role holder.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
