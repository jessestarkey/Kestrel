## Design Intent
The program manager is the government system owner, under the AO. The information owners are those CCSD Section 2 names, with the intelligence mission leads owning the mission data. The PMO ISSE owns the security design and Certification to Field. The performer ISSM owns the authorization package, and the ISSOs own day-to-day compliance. The SCA assesses, and the PSO owns personnel and physical security. The foreign disclosure officer, the privacy officer, the supply chain lead, the CSSP, and each team lead hold their named roles. A responsibility matrix in the SSP records who decides what. It records that the Security Team and the CSSP hold no administrative roles beyond the exceptions in Privileged Access Control (`ID-4.1`). It is reviewed each year and on any change of role holder, organization, contract, or boundary.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
