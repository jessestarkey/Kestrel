## Design Intent
Every control is assessed at least once in a three-year cycle, with a third of the families each year. Controls that change often, and the High-integrity key, signing, and datalink controls, are assessed each quarter or continuously. The SCA runs the independent assessments, and the ISSOs run self-assessments between them. Controls inherited from the cloud provider's IL5 authorization and the other providers in Inherited Control Validation (`GV-5.12`) are accepted on each provider's current assessment. Completion is tracked, so a slipped cycle reaches the AO.

## External Dependencies
The SCA, who runs the independent assessments.
