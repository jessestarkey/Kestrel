## Design Intent
Every control is assessed at least once in a three-year cycle, with a third of the families each year. Controls that change often, such as identity, configuration, vulnerability, and AI model controls, are assessed each quarter or continuously. The SCA runs the independent assessments, and the ISSOs run self-assessments between them. Completion is tracked, so a slipped cycle reaches the AO.

## External Dependencies
The SCA, who runs the independent assessments.
