## Design Intent
Each model's robustness requirements come from its threat model and how much the mission relies on it. For the detection models, that means holding detection rates under weather, sensor degradation, camouflage, and RF deception. For language model use cases, it means resisting jailbreaks and injected instructions. The AISP states each requirement as a measurable threshold that validation tests against.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
