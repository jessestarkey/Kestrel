## Design Intent
Correlate badge events at each KESTREL site with logical sign-ins, so an operator signed in at a station but not badged into that site raises an alert. The PSO owns the physical access control systems, and the parent organization's facility those at the alternate site. KESTREL consumes their events and sends badge requests from its account lifecycle, as the facility agreement allows.

## External Dependencies
The PSO's physical access control systems at the data center and each operating site, and the parent organization's facility access control system at the alternate site, under the facility agreement.
