## Design Intent
Severity is computed from asset criticality, data sensitivity, scope, and whether the incident can affect an aircraft. The AI incident taxonomy adds training data poisoning, prompt injection, including through retrieved reports, model theft, jailbreak abuse, and AI agent misuse. It also covers personal information leaking through model output, concept drift, a tampered or misbehaving onboard edge model, and a compromised foundation model deployment. Each severity has its own paging and escalation route, and a response metrics dashboard tracks time to triage, contain, and recover.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
