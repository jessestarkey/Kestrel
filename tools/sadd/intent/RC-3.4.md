## Design Intent
The alternate site is a parent organization facility that hosts KESTREL's warm standby, a second HSM, and a copy of the backups. A second dedicated circuit on a separate path links it to the cloud. The agreement names who may activate it and how quickly it must accept the workload.

## External Dependencies
The parent organization, which hosts KESTREL's alternate site under a facility agreement. The circuit provider, which supplies the second dedicated circuit on a separate path.
