## Design Intent
The alternate site is a parent organization facility, far enough from the data center that no regional event reaches both. It hosts KESTREL's warm standbys, a second HSM, and a copy of the backups. If the data center is lost, its Tier 2 services are restored into recovery spokes in the second IL5 region, data lake first, with each mission team's zone in its own spoke. The data center's two circuits come from different carriers on diverse paths to different meet-me points, so no single carrier, path, or meet-me point isolates it. Every carrier agreement carries priority restoration and the carrier's contingency plan. Production traffic fails from one data center circuit to the other each quarter. The alternate site has its own circuit to the cloud hub, also over DISN, and the yearly failover exercise proves it. The facility agreement names who may activate the site, how quickly it accepts the workload, and KESTREL's priority access in an area-wide emergency.

## External Dependencies
The parent organization, which hosts KESTREL's alternate site under a facility agreement. The circuit carriers. DISA, which carries the circuits over DISN through the access point's meet-me points. The IL5 cloud provider, whose second region holds the Tier 2 recovery spokes under its provisional authorization.
