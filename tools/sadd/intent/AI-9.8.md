## Design Intent
Retiring a model deletes its weights and the embeddings derived from it, revokes its endpoint identities, and removes it from every aircraft. The IL6 enclave's owner is told to retire its baseline copies, and the provider confirms deletion of a retired foundation model deployment. Its record is kept for as long as its certification evidence must be. The AISP sets the disposal method for each asset type.

## External Dependencies
The IL6 enclave's owner, which retires its baseline copies. The IL5 cloud provider, which confirms deletion.
