## Design Intent
Proof every person before KESTREL issues an account, at the assurance level the AMP sets for each population. DoD users are proofed through their CAC issuance, which also confirms their address, so KESTREL binds them to that record rather than re-proofing them. The program office sponsors each performer's CAC, and that sponsorship is the nomination KESTREL requires. CAC affiliation marks contractors and a nationality attribute marks partners, so roles can tell them apart.

## External Dependencies
DoD PKI CAC issuance, which proofs DoD users before they reach KESTREL. The program office's contractor CAC sponsorship with DMDC.
