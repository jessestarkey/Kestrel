## Design Intent
Proof every person before KESTREL issues an account, at the assurance level the AMP sets for each population. DoD users are proofed through their CAC issuance, so KESTREL binds them to that record rather than re-proofing them. Operators and platform administrators sit at the highest assurance level, since they can command aircraft or change the boundary.

## External Dependencies
DoD PKI CAC issuance, which proofs DoD users before they reach KESTREL.
