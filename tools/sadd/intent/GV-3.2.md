## Design Intent
The clause library carries DFARS 252.204-7012 and the CMMC clauses, the cloud computing clause, SBOM delivery, vulnerability disclosure, incident reporting timelines, CNSA 2.0 support, and data rights terms for each payload vendor. It also carries the acquisition security requirements, namely functional properties, design information, and the developer's configuration and supply chain duties. The supply chain lead verifies each contract carries the clauses its tier requires, flows them down, and monitors compliance each year, not only at renewal. CUI held by the performer and the penetration testing provider is covered under their clauses.

## External Dependencies
The performer and every subcontractor, which carry the flowed-down clauses.
