## Design Intent
The clause library carries DFARS 252.204-7012 and the CMMC clauses, the cloud computing clause, and SBOM delivery. It also carries vulnerability disclosure, incident reporting timelines, and data rights terms for each payload vendor. The supply chain lead verifies that each contract carries the clauses its tier requires and that the prime contract flows them down to subcontractors. The performer's CMMC Level 2 status covers CUI held on its laptops, and it is rechecked at each renewal.

## External Dependencies
The performer and every subcontractor, which carry the flowed-down clauses.
