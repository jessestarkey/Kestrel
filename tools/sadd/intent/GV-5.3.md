## Design Intent
The ISSM authors KESTREL's SSP with the ISSOs, recording what is built against each SADD outcome. The PMO ISSE reviews it. The AO approves an initial SSP before the interim authorization to test, from the CCSD and SADD, and the full SSP with the package. It is maintained in OSCAL, so the SCTM and assessment tools can read it, and it is revised with every significant change.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
