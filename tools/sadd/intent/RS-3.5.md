## Design Intent
The likeliest spills are classified content arriving through a high-to-low product, caught by KESTREL's content and marking checks, or pasted into a language model prompt. The ISSM traces every copy, namely index entries, embeddings, training snapshots, and models trained on them. Copies also include onboard models, IL6 baselines, prompt and response logs, the CSSP's retained telemetry, and backups at the alternate site and in the second region. The Data Team, Platform Team, ML Team, IT Operations, and Fleet Operations for onboard models suspend access and sanitize each copy. Cloud copies are cryptographically erased. A locked backup is sanitized by destroying its backup set's key, since retention lock prevents deletion. Every affected copy stays held until the PSO and the AO release it. The ISSM reports the spill out of band to the AO, the PSO, the original classification authority, the CSSP, and the IL6 enclave's owner.

## External Dependencies
The IL6 enclave's owner, whose release review and security office join any spill traced to a high-to-low product. The PSO, who receives every spill report. The IL5 cloud provider, which supports sanitizing cloud storage and model deployments.
