## Design Intent
KESTREL's label set combines the CUI category, dissemination markings such as releasability to partner nations, the owning mission team, and the payload vendor whose terms apply. The Data Team labels every record at ingest and keeps the catalog. Discovery scans run across the data lake zones, the cloud databases, the report index, the service desk, and backups. They find anything unlabeled or labeled inconsistently with its content.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
