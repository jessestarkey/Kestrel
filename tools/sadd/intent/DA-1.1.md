## Design Intent
KESTREL's label set combines the CUI category, dissemination markings such as releasability to partner nations, the owning mission team, and the payload vendor whose terms apply. The Data Team labels every record at ingest, including prior collection over F28, writing labels as object metadata, and keeps the catalog. Content classifiers scan the databases and structured files. Imagery and recordings are checked by their labels against the label inventory. Discovery covers every store, namely the data lake zones, ML Dev/Test, the model registry, the cloud databases, the report index, the service desk, the security monitoring platform, backups, and the fleet's storage modules. Each result is checked against CCSD Section 2, and a store or type with no row is a finding.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
