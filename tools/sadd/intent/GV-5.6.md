## Design Intent
Every system-to-system exchange in the system description has a signed interconnection agreement documenting both sides' interfaces and controls. These cover the CSSP, the cross domain service provider with the IL6 enclave's AO as a party, and each partner nation. They also cover the performer, the parent organization's managed devices, DoD PKI and the External Certificate Authorities, the DNS threat feed vendor, and the paging service. Each agreement also covers onward exchanges, such as partner dissemination and CSSP reporting. Public package indexes get no agreement, since the mirror only pulls from them. Provider authorizations sit under Inherited Control Validation (`GV-5.12`), and contracts under Contract Security Requirements (`GV-3.2`). Each agreement is reviewed each year and whenever its connection changes.

## External Dependencies
Each signatory to an agreement, namely the CSSP, the cross domain service provider, the IL6 enclave's AO, and each partner nation. Also the performer, the parent organization, DoD PKI and the External Certificate Authorities, the DNS threat feed vendor, and the paging service.
