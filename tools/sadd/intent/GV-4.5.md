## Design Intent
The PMO ISSE maps KESTREL's AI use cases against the DoD AI Cybersecurity Risk Management Tailoring Guide, the NIST AI RMF, and the DoD Responsible AI strategy. Gaps are traced to the AI outcomes that close them. A new or revised AI directive is assessed within a quarter of issue.

## External Dependencies
DoD and NIST, whose AI guidance this mapping tracks.
