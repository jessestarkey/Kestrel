## Design Intent
Platform service baselines cover the spoke secrets vaults, the feedback endpoints' queues and functions, the portal database service, and the foundation model deployment. The foundation model's baseline requires a private endpoint and provider-side prompt logging disabled. Application baselines cover how the mission teams' applications are hosted, along with the service desk platform and Payload Vendor B's conversion software, which follow their vendors' guidance.

## External Dependencies
The IL5 cloud provider, whose controls KESTREL inherits under its provisional authorization. Payload Vendor B publishes the deployment guidance for its conversion software.
