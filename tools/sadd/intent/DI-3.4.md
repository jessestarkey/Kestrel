## Design Intent
Platform service baselines cover every managed service, such as the spoke secrets vaults, the feedback endpoints' queues and functions, the database services, and each foundation model deployment. Every foundation model deployment's baseline requires a private endpoint and provider-side prompt logging disabled. Application baselines cover how the mission teams' applications are hosted, and COTS products such as the service desk platform and the payload vendors' conversion software follow their vendors' guidance.

## External Dependencies
The IL5 cloud provider, whose controls KESTREL inherits under its provisional authorization. Payload Vendor B publishes the deployment guidance for its conversion software.
