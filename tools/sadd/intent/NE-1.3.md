## Design Intent
Flow telemetry from every firewall and the SDN fabric is checked for cleartext and obsolete protocols, such as Telnet, FTP, SMBv1, unsigned LDAP, and TLS below 1.2. Each one found is disabled. In the ground segment, the passive sensors find them without probing, and any the manufacturer requires get compensating controls. The payload processing workstation connects only if the AO re-dates CCSD tailoring action 3. Its transfer would then be the one registered exception, confined to its conduit.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
