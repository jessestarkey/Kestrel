## Design Intent
Every change process feeds one Security Impact Analysis intake, namely asset, network, identity, application release, model and prompt, and fleet maintenance changes. Pipeline releases and retraining within the approved envelope get a standing classification recorded automatically. Anything else goes to the ISSM, who asks whether it alters anything the authorization was granted against. Significant changes go to the SCA and the AO before they are made. Each analysis flags the SSP, SCTM, and CCSD updates it needs, and each change's controls are verified afterward.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
