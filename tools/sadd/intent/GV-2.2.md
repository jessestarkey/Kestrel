## Design Intent
A team requests an exception through the service desk, naming the requirement, the reason, the compensating measures, and an end date. The ISSM assesses the risk. Exceptions that affect a control in the authorization go to the AO, as CCSD tailoring action 3 did. Expired exceptions alert the ISSM, and exception trends are reported to the steering committee each quarter.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
