## Design Intent
A team requests an exception through the service desk, naming the requirement, the reason, the compensating measures, and an end date. The ISSM assesses the risk, and approval follows Risk Acceptance Process (`RK-1.4`). The PMO ISSE may therefore approve only Low exceptions outside CCSD rows 6, 8, 10, and 13, and the AO approves the rest. At expiry an exception is remediated, re-justified, or escalated, and an expired one becomes an open finding. Exception trends are reported to the steering committee each quarter.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
