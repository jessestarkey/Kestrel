## Design Intent
Build entitlements as bundles for each user role in the system description, scoped to the team that role belongs to. Imagery analysts get Mission Team 1's raw collection and detections, signals analysts get Mission Team 2's, and both get the shared reports. IT Operations provisions accounts, but the approval comes from the requester's product owner or team lead, so no one approves and provisions the same access. The operator bundle includes approving the mission-planning agent's drafts, so that approval is an entitlement that can be reviewed rather than a feature anyone can reach.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
