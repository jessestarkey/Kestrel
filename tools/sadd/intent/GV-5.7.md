## Design Intent
The ISSM submits the package to the AO, and AO determinations and conditions are tracked to closure in the POA&M. Before full authorization, an interim authorization to test covers Phases 1 to 3. It covers integration on spare aircraft and the simulator, the access point and CSSP connections from Phase 1, and the government-furnished prior collection the AO admits over F28 for training. It names its duration and connections, and it ends at the ATO decision or on any condition the AO sets.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
