## Design Intent
The AO appoints the performer ISSM, and the ISSM appoints each ISSO, each by a signed letter. Each letter names the person, the role, the KESTREL boundary it covers, the authority it confers, and its effective date. The ISSM tracks every letter and reissues it whenever a role holder or the boundary changes, such as when the alternate site was added.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
