## Design Intent
The ISSM keeps one risk register that receives risks from every source. Sources include threat models and the architecture risk register, penetration tests, the SCA, continuous monitoring, AI certification acceptances, and supply chain assessments. Each risk carries an owner, a score, a treatment, and a review date, and links to its POA&M items and SCTM controls. The POA&M lives in eMASS and the register in the program's work tracker, linked once by record ID. The steering committee reviews the register each month, and a risk posture dashboard shows it to leadership.

## External Dependencies
DISA's eMASS, which holds the POA&M.
