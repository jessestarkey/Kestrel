## Design Intent
An auditor appointed by the program manager, independent of the performer teams, audits whether KESTREL's plans and procedures are carried out as written. Audits cover areas such as change records, access reviews, backup restore tests, and AI board decisions. The audit schedule reaches every governing plan at least once every two years. Findings are tracked until the fix is verified, and the audit charter is reviewed each year.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
