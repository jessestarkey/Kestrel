## Design Intent
The parent organization's internal review office, outside the program's reporting line, audits whether KESTREL's plans and procedures are carried out as written. Audits cover areas such as change records, access reviews, backup restore tests, and AI board decisions. The audit schedule reaches every governing plan at least once every two years. Findings are tracked until the fix is verified, and the audit charter is reviewed each year.

## External Dependencies
The parent organization's internal review office, which conducts the audits.
