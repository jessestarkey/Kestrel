## Design Intent
The Security Team runs KESTREL's threat intelligence function, with a named analyst role. Its requirements focus on adversaries targeting ISR and unmanned systems, attacks on datalinks and navigation, and attacks on AI supply chains and models. Sources are the CSSP, DoD intelligence products, and the performer's DIB threat sharing. The Security Team reviews the program and its sources each year.

## External Dependencies
The CSSP and DoD intelligence producers, which supply threat intelligence. DC3's DIB Cybersecurity program, through the performer.
