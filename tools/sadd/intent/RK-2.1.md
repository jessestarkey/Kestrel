## Design Intent
The Security Team runs KESTREL's threat intelligence function, with a named threat intelligence analyst. Its requirements focus on adversaries targeting ISR and unmanned systems, attacks on datalinks and navigation, and attacks on AI supply chains and models. Sources are the CSSP and DoD intelligence products, much of them classified. A cleared government point of contact and the CSSP provide sanitized products, and only releasable content enters KESTREL. The Security Team reviews the program and its sources each year.

## External Dependencies
The CSSP and DoD intelligence producers, which supply threat intelligence through a cleared government point of contact.
