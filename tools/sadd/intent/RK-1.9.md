## Design Intent
The chief engineer and the PMO ISSE watch every technology class likely to reach KESTREL. Today those are new foundation models and agent frameworks, new payload sensors, and new datalink and satellite communications. Each one that reaches an adoption decision is assessed first. AI candidates go through the AI/ML Governance Review Board, and everything else through the ISSM's Security Impact Analysis (SIA) (`GV-5.11`). Risks the current controls do not cover go into the register. The post-quantum transition is a mandate, owned by Post-Quantum Cryptography Readiness (`DA-3.2`).

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
