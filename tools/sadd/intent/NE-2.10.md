## Design Intent
KESTREL uses the enterprise cross domain solution a DoD cross domain service provider operates, and that provider owns product selection and accreditation. Low-to-high baselines need Certification to Field approval before the transfer gateway stages them. High-to-low products pass the enclave's release review and then KESTREL's own content checks. The provider's transfer logs and the gateway's logs both go to the security monitoring platform. Manual media transfer across the domains is prohibited.

## External Dependencies
The enterprise cross domain service provider, under the interconnection agreement covering both directions. The IL6 enclave's owner, whose release review approves every high-to-low product.
