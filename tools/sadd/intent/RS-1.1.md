## Design Intent
Runbooks cover the cloud provider's own incident types, such as a compromised subscription, an exposed storage service, or a stolen workload identity, using the provider's native response actions. AI/ML runbooks cover a poisoned training snapshot, prompt injection through retrieved reports, a compromised or misbehaving AI agent, and a tampered or misbehaving onboard edge model. They also cover a foundation model incident on the provider's side. A lost aircraft is treated as exposing its model and datalink module, and its datalink keys are superseded fleet-wide. Each runbook names the team that executes it.

## External Dependencies
The IL5 cloud provider, whose native threat protection and response actions the cloud runbooks use.
