## Design Intent
Runbooks cover the cloud provider's own incident types, such as a compromised subscription, an exposed storage service, or a stolen workload identity. They use the provider's native response actions. AI/ML runbooks cover a poisoned training snapshot, a compromised or misbehaving AI agent, a foundation model incident on the provider's side, and a lost aircraft carrying an onboard model. Each runbook names the team that executes it.

## External Dependencies
The IL5 cloud provider, whose native threat protection and response actions the cloud runbooks use.
