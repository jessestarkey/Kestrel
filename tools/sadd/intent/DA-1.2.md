## Design Intent
One policy decides data access from user and data attributes, namely team, payload vendor rights, role, partner nation, and the record's labels. Team and role come from the identity provider, vendor rights from the Data Team's catalog, partner nation from partner sponsorship, and releasability from the foreign disclosure officer's approval. A policy engine replica on each side of the circuit fails closed. Its enforcement points are every application, the partner API, each data lake zone, and the report index's retrieval. The payload vendor split is enforced like a compartment, and flows between zones are allowed only where F18 permits. Policy changes are tested against fixed access cases before deployment.

## External Dependencies
The payload vendors, whose data rights terms the vendor attributes encode.
