## Design Intent
The business impact analysis works through KESTREL's mission processes, namely flying and controlling the aircraft, collecting and ingesting data, detecting and analyzing, releasing to partners, and retraining models. The intelligence mission leads and Fleet Operations set how long each process can be lost. The results set the criticality tiers that Recovery's recovery objectives and the incident severity model use. The analysis is reviewed each year and after any significant change.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
