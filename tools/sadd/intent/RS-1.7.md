## Design Intent
The CSSP's service agreement divides the work. The CSSP monitors around the clock, triages, and supplies threat intelligence. The CSSP runs the pre-approved containment playbooks when an incident cannot wait for the Security Team, and the Security Team carries out every other response action inside KESTREL. The security monitoring platform forwards all telemetry to the CSSP. The CSSP's analysts also read that telemetry and the cloud's native threat protection in place through their host pool. The ISSM receives the CSSP's directives and advisories and tracks each one to completion.

## External Dependencies
The CSSP, under the detection and response split in its service agreement.
