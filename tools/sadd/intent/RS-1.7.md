## Design Intent
The CSSP's service agreement divides the work. The CSSP monitors around the clock, triages, and supplies threat intelligence. The Security Team carries out every response action inside KESTREL. The security monitoring platform forwards all telemetry to the CSSP, and the CSSP's analysts read it in place through their host pool. The ISSM receives the CSSP's directives and advisories and tracks each one to completion.

## External Dependencies
The CSSP, under the detection and response split in its service agreement.
