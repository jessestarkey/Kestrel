## Design Intent
The command and payload datalinks are KESTREL's only authorized wireless. No wireless LAN exists in the data center or at the operating sites, and embedded radios are disabled on every server, workstation, and station. WPA2/3-Enterprise therefore has nothing to authenticate today. Wireless sensors at the data center, the alternate site, and each operating site look for rogue access points and alert the Security Team.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
