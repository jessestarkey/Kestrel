## Design Intent
The identity provider's sign-in and directory logs and the cloud tenant's entitlement changes all feed the security monitoring platform. Risky sign-ins include a partner from an unexpected network, an operator signed in at a station they are not badged into, and a performer laptop that suddenly reports as non-compliant. A compromised account is disabled at once, and an identity posture score is reported each month.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
