## Design Intent
The identity provider's sign-in and directory logs and the cloud tenant's entitlement changes all feed the security monitoring platform. Risky sign-ins include a partner from an unexpected network, a performer laptop that suddenly reports as non-compliant, and the badge correlation of Physical and Logical Access Convergence (`ID-1.10`). The exposure and leak monitoring service checks breach sources for KESTREL credentials and alerts on a match. The Security Team confirms each event class is present and reviews them weekly. A compromised account is contained under Automated Containment and Isolation Procedures (`RS-3.2`), where the CSSP may disable it at once through its playbook. An identity posture score is reported each month.

## External Dependencies
The PSO's physical access control systems, through ID-1.10. The performer's device management, which supplies each laptop's compliance signal. The exposure and leak monitoring service.
