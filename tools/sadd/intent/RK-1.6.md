## Design Intent
The SCA writes the SAR, recording a determination and its evidence for every control assessed, and checks its coverage against the Security Assessment Plan. The ISSM checks it for factual accuracy without changing any determination. It is marked and handled as CUI and distributed only to the AO, the program manager as system owner, the ISSM, and the PMO ISSE. It goes into the package the ISSM submits.

## External Dependencies
The SCA, who assesses KESTREL independently for the AO. DISA's eMASS, which holds the SAR.
