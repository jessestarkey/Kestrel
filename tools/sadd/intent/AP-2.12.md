## Design Intent
Contracts with every software supplier require an SBOM with each delivery and each update, such as the payload vendors' and the service desk vendor's. Each SBOM is checked for format and completeness and recorded against its product in the inventory. Where a supplier cannot provide one, the gap is recorded and the product's monitoring is tightened. Fleet suppliers' SBOMs follow Secure OT/Legacy Device Baselines (`DI-3.5`).

## External Dependencies
The payload vendors, the service desk vendor, and other COTS suppliers, which supply SBOMs under their contracts.
