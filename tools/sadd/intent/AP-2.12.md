## Design Intent
Contracts with every software supplier require an SBOM with each delivery and each update. That includes the payload vendors, the service desk vendor, and the ground equipment manufacturers. Each SBOM is checked for format and completeness and recorded against its product in the inventory. Where a supplier cannot provide one, the gap is recorded and the product's monitoring is tightened.

## External Dependencies
The payload vendors, the service desk vendor, and the aircraft and ground equipment manufacturers, which supply SBOMs under their contracts.
