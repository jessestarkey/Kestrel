## Design Intent
KESTREL maintains a privacy impact assessment on DD Form 2930. It is reassessed whenever a new collection, sensor, AI use case, or partner release would process personal information. Incidental imagery is never retrieved by personal identifier, so it forms no system of records. User account and service desk records are retrieved by identifier and fall under the DoD-wide SORN the component privacy office names in the PIA. The account request form carries a Privacy Act statement drawn from the PIA. Privacy Requirements Definition (`AI-1.2`) bars training any model to identify individuals. Lifting that bar needs the AI/ML Governance Review Board, a revised PIA, and a CCSD revision. Any change that would retrieve imagery by person, or raise the overlay tier, also triggers CCSD revision.

## External Dependencies
The DoD component's privacy office, which reviews and approves each assessment.
