## Design Intent
KESTREL maintains a privacy impact assessment on DD Form 2930. It is reassessed whenever a new collection, sensor, AI use case, or partner release would process personal information. Incidental imagery is never retrieved by personal identifier, so KESTREL operates no system of records. Any capability that would retrieve by person must complete a new assessment first. The responsible AI policy prohibits identifying individuals, so such a capability also needs AI/ML Governance Review Board approval.

## External Dependencies
The DoD component's privacy office, which reviews and approves each assessment.
