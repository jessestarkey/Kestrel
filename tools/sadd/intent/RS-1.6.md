## Design Intent
The ISSM notifies the AO, and the CSSP reports to the DoD reporting chain within the timelines CJCSM 6510.01B sets. If partner data is affected, the foreign disclosure officer notifies the partner nations under their agreements. Incidents involving a vendor's product are coordinated with that vendor. A breach of personal information is reported through the privacy officer to the DoD component privacy office within its timelines. When a performer laptop holding KESTREL source code is involved, the performer also reports to DC3 under DFARS 252.204-7012.

## External Dependencies
The CSSP and the DoD reporting chain it reports to. The partner nations, under their memoranda of agreement. The DoD component privacy office. The performer and DC3, under DFARS 252.204-7012. Each vendor, under its support agreement.
