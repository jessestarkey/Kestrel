## Design Intent
The ISSM notifies the AO, and the CSSP reports to the DoD reporting chain within the timelines CJCSM 6510.01B sets. If partner data is affected, the foreign disclosure officer notifies the partner nations under their agreements. Incidents involving a vendor's product are coordinated with that vendor. When a performer laptop holding KESTREL source code is involved, the performer also reports under DFARS 252.204-7012.

## External Dependencies
The CSSP and the DoD reporting chain it reports to. The partner nations, under their memoranda of agreement. The performer, under DFARS 252.204-7012. Each vendor, under its support agreement.
