## Design Intent
The pipeline combines each release's findings into one risk score against thresholds the PMO ISSE sets in the ASP. A release within the thresholds proceeds on the pipeline's evidence. A release outside them goes to the PMO ISSE, who approves it, sends it back, or routes it for acceptance under Risk Acceptance Process (`RK-1.4`). Every release is CCSD row 10, so any residual risk above Very Low goes to the AO.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
