## Design Intent
The AI use case inventory lists every AI use case KESTREL runs or has proposed, with its sponsor, models, data, any actions it can take, and its risk tier. Today those are the onboard and cloud detection models, the embedding model, the person-detection model, the LLM analyst assistant, the mission-planning agent, and AI-assisted alert triage. The risk tier rises with mission impact and with any ability to act. Each quarter a discovery sweep checks procurement records, cloud AI service usage, and AI features vendors have switched on in owned products, against the model registry.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
