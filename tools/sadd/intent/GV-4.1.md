## Design Intent
The AI use case inventory lists every AI use case KESTREL runs or has proposed, with its sponsor, models, data, any actions it can take, and its risk tier. Today those are the onboard and cloud detection models, the embedding model, the LLM analyst assistant, and the mission-planning agent. The risk tier rises with mission impact and with any ability to act, such as drafting flight plans. The inventory is reviewed each quarter against the model registry.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
