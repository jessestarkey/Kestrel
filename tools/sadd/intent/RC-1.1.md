## Design Intent
Backup coverage follows three criticality tiers. Tier 1 holds the identity provider, the PKI and HSM backups, the hubs' rule base, and the ground segment's configuration. Tier 2 holds the data lake, the model registry, the mission teams' applications and databases, and the security monitoring platform. Tier 3 holds dev/test, training scratch, and experimentation. The coverage list also includes the system's security documentation. Gaps across supported, unsupported, and OT systems are reviewed each year.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
