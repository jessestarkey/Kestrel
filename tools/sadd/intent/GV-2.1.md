## Design Intent
Policies and plans are written and versioned in the program's document repository, reviewed by the ISSM, and approved by the Configuration Control Board. Each is reviewed every year, and earlier when an incident, assessment, or significant change calls for it. Superseded versions are retired and archived. Policies a machine can enforce, such as the hub rule base, admission rules, and landing zone guardrails, are written as policy as code.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
