## Design Intent
Policies and plans are written and versioned in the program's document repository and reviewed by the ISSM. The program manager signs the system security policy. The Configuration Control Board approves standards and governing plans, except those the AO approves, such as the ConMon plan. The ISSM approves procedures and runbooks. Each is reviewed every year, and earlier when an incident, assessment, or significant change calls for it. Superseded versions are retired and archived, and policies a machine can enforce are written as policy as code.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
