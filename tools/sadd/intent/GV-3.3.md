## Design Intent
The supply chain lead assesses KESTREL's software and hardware supply chain each year and maps the dependencies the mission rests on. These include the aircraft manufacturer, the two payload vendors, the cloud provider, the foundation model's publisher, and the HSM vendor. Single points of supply and countries of origin are recorded, and findings are tracked to closure.

## External Dependencies
The payload vendors, the aircraft and ground equipment manufacturers, the service desk vendor, the cloud provider, the CSSP, and the other suppliers in the vendor population.
