## Design Intent
The ML team threat-models every model and the mission teams threat-model every AI use case built on one, using MITRE ATLAS alongside the program's application methodology. The PMO ISSE reviews each model at the design gate. Models cover poisoning through feedback and federated updates, evasion by adversary camouflage and deception, prompt injection through retrieved reports, and capture of an aircraft's onboard model.

## External Dependencies
None outside the boundary.
